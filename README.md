## 1. Mission, precedence and decision rights

Develop the **existing Expo / React Native Web / TypeScript** project into a polished, responsive **classroom demonstration** of CurRENT, a FinTech-enabled peer-to-peer item-rental marketplace serving Klang Valley, Malaysia. Use **one application, one current feature/UI version**, exactly two customer-facing dashboards (**Renter** and **Lender**), and two isolated account/data contexts (**Normal account** and **Demo Account / Admin Demo data**), selected **only by secure login**, never via an in-app mode switch. There must not be a separate or outdated customer-facing demo application, duplicate mode-specific page implementation, or demo-only replacement for a broken Normal Mode feature. **Admin Demo is fictional presentation mode**, never a third dashboard, staff role, risk-approval console, or grant of real privileged access.

**Single source of truth:** CurRENT platform rules govern eligibility, risk bands, instant booking, deposits, cancellation, scoring, disputes and final claim outcomes. A lender controls their own listing description, price, private address, public general service area, photos/video, future *unreserved* availability, at least two selectable rental-start-time windows, and fulfilment of confirmed delivery-only orders. A lender **cannot approve, reject, veto, pause, or unilaterally cancel an eligible already-confirmed booking**, adjudicate a score-0–29 request, set risk scores, decide claim liability, or release a deposit. Lender/renter receipt, check-in, check-out and condition-evidence actions **are acknowledgements/evidence, never permission for a booking to become Active**. CurRENT itself is a classroom simulation, not a courier, insurer, bank or lender.

**Precedence:** (1) founder-confirmed requirements in **this V3.23 consolidated document once approved**; (2) visual/design reference `CurRENT Website.pdf` where not superseded; (3) conceptual `CurRENT.pdf` and planning workbook `CurRENT.xlsx`; (4) prior code and older README. The updated screenshot, the eight founder change requests and their four clarifications are integrated into this document, **not a second conflicting addendum**. Earlier website and V1.8 values cannot override later choices. Preserve compatible working software and genuine data.

**Real vs simulated:** Real app authentication, shared durable listings, saved items, media uploads, owner calendars, booking and evidence states, notifications, messaging, history and calculations must work across authorised devices via a shared backend. **Simulate** eKYC outcomes, face/liveness/document checks, FPX payment confirmation, funds held/released/refunded, commission/payouts, insurance/protection, BNPL concepts and claim decisions. The identity-document `Upload Photo` UI is a **functional local-only preview for fictional/sample images** and the face scan is a **visual demo**, not a live KYC process. Never collect, transmit or persist real identity documents or biometric images in the classroom demo; never collect bank credentials or actual money, or imply live insurer, courier, BNPL, risk-model or payment-provider integration. Label fictional actions/records `Demo` clearly without drowning customers in technical disclaimers.

**Normative hierarchy:** Sections **4–12** define mandatory account, media, scheduling, financial and lifecycle rules; Sections **2–3 and 13–15** govern information architecture, catalogue, UI and demo presentations. ## 11. Delivery-based check-in, check-out, disputes, liability, debt and cancellation

### 11.1 Renter condition evidence, lender shared-media review
