## 1. Mission, precedence and decision rights

Develop the **existing Expo / React Native Web / TypeScript** project into a polished responsive **classroom demonstration** of CurRENT, a peer-to-peer item-rental marketplace for Klang Valley, Malaysia. Use **one app** with two customer-facing dashboards (**Renter** and **Lender**) and two data experiences (**Normal Mode** and **Admin Demo**). Admin Demo is a **clearly labelled fictional presentation mode**, NOT an Admin Dashboard, staff portal, operational risk-review console or extra role with privileged access.

**Authority, once and for all:** CurRENT platform rules govern verification, risk eligibility, instant reservation, deposit rates, cancellations, score adjustments, disputes and final claim outcomes. Lenders manage **their own listing description, price, future unreserved availability, at least two selectable scheduled rental-start-time windows and fulfilment of delivery-only orders**. Lenders **cannot approve, reject, veto, pause or unilaterally cancel an eligible already-confirmed booking**, decide a score-0–29 request, assign scores or determine claim liability/deposit release. **No physical handover or activation approvals exist.** Lenders may report delivered/returned item condition or disputes as factual evidence, not booking permission. The app is a classroom demo: it does not run a real courier network or guarantee delivery.

**Precedence:** (1) founder-confirmed requirements in **this V3.22 document**; (2) visual/design reference `CurRENT Website.pdf` where not superseded; (3) the conceptual `CurRENT.pdf` and financial `CurRENT.xlsx`; (4) old repository/README behaviour. Later decisions override old V1.8 and early website figures: first verified score is **75, Medium**, Low begins at **80**, and ordinary bookings never await owner approval. Preserve working software where compatible; **do not append or revive obsolete specifications**.

**Real vs simulated:** Real sign-in sessions, shared persisted listings, messaging, calendar availability, booking states, item photos, **two-party return-condition confirmation**, receipts/history and calculations. **Simulate** eKYC outcomes, payment, deposit holding/release, refunds, commission payouts, insurance/referrals, BNPL concepts and claim awards. Never process real cards/transfers, collect real identity documents, imply active insurance, or require credentials for financial services. Use visible but unobtrusive `Demo` disclosures. The app must work across devices via a shared backend, not just local browser storage.

**How to interpret this specification:** Sections **4–12 are the normative data/business rules** (eligibility, scheduling, money, lifecycle, disputes); Sections **2–3 and 13–15 specify UI and seeded presentation data**, and Section 17 supplies acceptance examples, **not alternative business rules**. ## 6. Charges, deposit calculation, checkout quote and protection

Use a **single tested financial rules module**, exact decimals or integer sen, and MYR two-decimal displays. All amounts below are simulated; quote snapshots must be immutable per confirmed booking, including score/deposit tier at booking.

```text
scheduledDurationMinutes = scheduledRentalEndAt - scheduledRentalStartAt  // positive
billableDays = max(1, ceil(scheduledDurationMinutes / 1440))  // started 24-hour blocks
rentalFee = dailyRate * billableDays
renterServiceFee = max(RM5, roundToSen(0.05 * rentalFee))
lenderCommission = roundToSen(0.12 * rentalFee)
lenderPayout = rentalFee - lenderCommission    // 88% of rental fee
securityDeposit = min(roundToSen(itemReplacementValue * riskTierRate), riskTierCap)
checkoutTotal = rentalFee + renterServiceFee + securityDeposit
consecutiveExtensionTotal = extraRentalFee + extraServiceFee  // RM0 new deposit
```

**Example:** Sony camera RM45/day, illustrative replacement value **RM4,000**; new verified renter **75 Medium**; **48h** = 2 billable days → rent **RM90**, renter fee **RM5**, deposit **RM400**, total **RM495 (Demo)**; lender commission **RM10.80**, lender illustrative payout **RM79.20**. The older **RM295** example applies **only to an already-Low renter (80+)**, whose deposit is RM200. For an RM12,000 item the deposit caps are **Low RM500 / Medium RM1,000 / High RM1,500**. Never claim the new 75 Medium renter owes RM200 or RM295.

**Workbook planning context, not runtime transactions:** The project forecasts approximately RM200 average rental value, RM2 **platform** insurance-referral income per completed rental, RM5 hypothetical BNPL-referral income, RM1.20 provider cost per rental and RM0.10 per SMS/OTP; illustrative Year-1 registrations ≈4,740 and completed rentals ≈2,795, estimated fixed costs ≈RM977,040/year and annual break-even volume ≈26,990 completed rentals. These are **forecasts**, not app revenue ledgers, operating insurance/BNPL services, extra checkout fees, seeded genuine users or requirements for an investor dashboard. If PDF/Excel forecast totals disagree slightly, don't invent a reconciliation or embed inconsistent totals in checkout; ask the founder if a commercial forecast feature is ever requested.

**Fee exclusions:** The deposit is refundable security, **not revenue**. Lender 12% commission is *deducted from rent*, not added to renter checkout. **Remove the unsupported `Demo item protection · RM5/day` toggle** and every associated calculation; the PDF/XLSX do not approve an RM5/day renter premium. A small `Item Protection (Demo Information)` explainer may describe a *future, conditional* partner concept (up to RM60,000 for eligible high-value items) without guaranteeing cover. ## 9. Canonical request, booking and return lifecycle (single source of truth)

**Different records:** A `pending_platform_review` **request** (Section 5) is **not** a booking and has **no** reserved interval, payment, deposit or lender decision. A direct-checkout **booking** exists only after the renter explicitly confirms simulated payment and the server atomically reserves the chosen interval. No intermediate owner-approval state exists. Do **not** confuse these two workflows or show a restricted application in normal Upcoming/Active rentals.

| Record / visible state | Entry rule | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending CurRENT Review (Demo)** | Score-0–29 renter submitted a valid request | Remains pending in `Platform Review Requests`; does not hold dates, accept payment or notify lender for approval. |
| **Confirmed — Upcoming (Demo)** | Eligible renter confirmed simulated payment and a future `[start,end)` slot was reserved | On reaching the selected start timestamp, becomes Active automatically. |
| **Active (Demo)** | Server time is **at or past scheduled start**, booking not cancelled, cleanly completed or disputed | Rental clock follows booked end; **Return Due** is a filter/subset for active rentals whose booked end has arrived and return is unresolved, not an independent paid booking. |
| **Return awaiting confirmation (substatus)** | Delivery-based return evidence and/or one party's no-issues acknowledgement received | The rental remains unresolved (shown under Active/Return Due as appropriate); deposit stays held until both parties confirm No Issues with evidence and no open dispute. This is **not a separate booking**. |
| **Completed — No Issues (Demo)** | Evidence is present and **both** parties separately confirm No Issues | Release full simulated deposit exactly once; apply at most one final score adjustment for the possession chain. |
| **Disputed / Under Platform Review (Demo)** | Either party raises an item/return dispute before clean settlement | Hold deposit; use only clearly fictional prescribed outcomes and appeals; do not route into the 0–29 `Platform Review Requests` tab. |
