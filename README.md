CurRENT is **Delivery Only**, so there is no physical pickup or in-person meeting.

CurRENT's Rental Risk Score ranges from 0 to 100, with four risk levels:

- **Low Risk:** 80–100

If an item arrives damaged, is not returned, or its condition is disputed, either party can report an issue through the relevant **Condition Evidence** section. The lender can also select **Apply Dispute**.

- Filing a dispute places the rental under review and keeps the security deposit on hold.
- CurRENT reviews the submitted condition photos, videos and other relevant information to determine liability.

Your Rental Risk Score starts at **80** after verification and changes based on your completed rentals:
